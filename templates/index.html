Automated Audit Pipeline

Fetch raw data straight from the Biometric & TimeChamp portals by date range — or upload files manually — then run the cross-reconciliation audit and open the styled workbook.

1 · Date Range & Sources

Both portals are downloaded and merged by the engine — same pipeline as a manual upload. Attendance up to yesterday is available.

Leave empty to use default recipients from Settings → Email delivery.

1 · Raw Punch & Timesheet Sources

upload_file

Drag & drop a file here…

Accepts .xls, .xlsx, and .csv. Pick one raw data file (Biometric, Timechamp, or Manual Tracking) — or several finished reports to consolidate.

    Leave empty to use default recipients from Settings → Email delivery.

    2 · Engine Verification Parameters

    • HR attendance cross-check Punches vs approved HR leave and holidays (live portal fetch).
    • Shift boundary audit Late logins past grace and early logouts flagged per day.
    • Worked-duration floors Days under 4h and under 8h worked flagged in the summary.
    • Break policy Excessive break hours and more than 4 pauses per day flagged.

    Built on this computer · large files can take a few minutes · the HR check needs internet.
